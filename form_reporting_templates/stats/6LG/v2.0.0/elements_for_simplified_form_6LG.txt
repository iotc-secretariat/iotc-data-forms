The Secretariat reviewed Form 6LG, developed in response to paragraph 3 of Recommendation 24/11 on marine pollution, with a view to simplifying the information collected on the recovery of abandoned, lost or discarded fishing gear.

The current form includes detailed information on fishing gear and FAD characteristics, habitat conditions and environmental impacts. To facilitate voluntary reporting by CPCs, the Secretariat proposes replacing the existing structure with a simplified dataset covering the date and location of recovery, type and quantity of gear recovered, and, where available, estimated weight and recovery outcome.

The WPDCS is invited to review the proposed minimum data requirements and provide advice on the revised reporting form.
