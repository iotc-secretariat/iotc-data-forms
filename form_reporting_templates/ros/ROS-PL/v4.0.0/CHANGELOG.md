# ROS PL reporting form — v4.0.0

## Source and version

Source: [IOTC Zenodo V4 release](https://zenodo.org/records/17509753).

Original filename: `Form-ROS-PL.xlsx`.

## Changes

- Revised the `META` worksheet to include reporting year, reporting entity, data source, and separate focal point and organisation contact details  

- Standardised identifiers across worksheets, replacing `OBSERVED_TRIP_NUMBER`, `SET_NUMBER`, `CATCH_NUMBER`, and `SPECIMEN_NUMBER` with `OBSERVED_TRIP_ID`, `SET_ID`, `CATCH_ID`, and `SPECIMEN_ID`, respectively  

- Simplified vessel information by removing contact details for vessels, owners and operators, as well as owner and charter/operator identification fields  

- Simplified vessel attributes by standardising measurement units for tonnage (GT), length overall (m), and fish storage capacity (m³), and removing free-text waste management fields  

- Updated vessel electronics terminology, replacing `GPS` with `GNSS`  


- Harmonised gillnet configuration fields, including mesh sizes, net dimensions, hanging ratio, net colours, floats, droplines and sinkers  

- Standardised fishing operation, catch and specimen field names, including species, sampling methods, processing types, estimation methods and mitigation measures  

- Revised specimen tagging information by replacing tag number fields with tag identifiers and separating the tag finder's name and contact details  

- Removed the two transshipment worksheets (`T-EVENTS` and `T-PRODUCTS`)  

- Harmonised worksheet structure, column headings and coded-field terminology across the form.
